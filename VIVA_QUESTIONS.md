# Viva Questions and Answers
1. What is an IDS? A system that detects and alerts on suspicious activity.
2. IDS versus IPS? IDS alerts; IPS can block.
3. Why ML? It can learn patterns from labelled examples.
4. What is a flow? A summary of related packets between endpoints.
5. Why Random Forest? It is robust, interpretable and handles nonlinear features.
6. What is training? Fitting a model on labelled examples.
7. What is testing? Measuring performance on unseen held-out data.
8. What is overfitting? Memorizing training data rather than generalizing.
9. Accuracy? Overall fraction of correct predictions.
10. Precision? Fraction of attack alerts that are correct.
11. Recall? Fraction of attacks found.
12. F1-score? Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
13. Confusion matrix? Counts of predicted versus actual classes.
14. False positive? Normal traffic incorrectly alerted.
15. False negative? Attack traffic missed.
16. Why stratify? It preserves class proportions in splits.
17. What is leakage? Test information influencing model fitting.
18. How is leakage prevented? Fit transforms on train data only.
19. Why impute? Models cannot generally consume missing values.
20. How are categories handled? One-hot encoding.
21. Why class weights? To reduce majority-class dominance.
22. What is CICIDS2017? A labelled intrusion-detection dataset.
23. What is UNSW-NB15? A modern labelled network-security dataset.
24. What is NSL-KDD? A benchmark intrusion dataset.
25. What is Scapy? A Python packet manipulation/capture library.
26. Why is Npcap needed? It enables packet capture on Windows.
27. Why SQLite? A simple local parameterized relational database.
28. Why Flask? A lightweight Python web framework.
29. Is confidence attack probability? No; it is a model score.
30. Is demo traffic malicious? No; it is safe synthetic data.
31. What does authorized monitoring mean? Capture only networks you own or may monitor.
32. Main limitation? Performance depends on representative labelled data.
